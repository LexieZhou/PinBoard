Critique my San Diego itinerary
Itinerary

📅 October 11 – Arrival + Easy Exploring

11:30 AM: Arrive, get rental car

1:30 PM: Check-in (Intercontinental SD), relax, freshen up

3:00 PM: Explore Waterfront: Seaport Village, Embarcadero

4:30 PM: Ballast Point Brewery

6:00 PM: Early dinner at Juniper & Ivy (reservations recommended)

Evening: Chill at hotel rooftop or short harbor walk

📅 October 12 – East County Adventure (Julian)

8:00 AM: Depart for Julian (1.5-2 hrs)

10:00 AM: Apple picking

11:30 AM: Mom’s Pie House

12:15 PM: Calico Cidery or Nickel Beer Co.

1:30 PM: Optional: Stonewall Peak trail (moderate hike—skip if tired)

4:00 PM: Head back to SD (1.5 hrs)

6:30 PM: Dinner at Animae (relaxing end to big day)

📅 October 13 – Balboa Park + Culture

9:00 AM: Balboa Park (museums, architecture)

11:00 AM: San Diego Zoo (spend 2–3 hours max)

2:30 PM: Mission Basilica San Diego de Alcalá (short visit)

3:30 PM: Old Town stroll, shops

5:00 PM: Tahona Bar (snacks + mezcal)

Evening: Relax at hotel or sunset walk

📅 October 14 – North Coast Drive

8:00 AM: Drive north via scenic coast

8:30 AM: Wayfarer Bread Co. (La Jolla)

9:30 AM: Torrey Pines hike (get there early for parking)

12:00 PM: Continue to Del Mar → Encinitas → Carlsbad (casual sightseeing, beach stops)

3:30 PM: Oceanside pier (quick visit)

6:00 PM: Dinner at Campfire (Carlsbad)

📅 October 15 – Beach Fun & Museums

9:00 AM: USS Midway Museum

11:30 AM: Lunch at Tribute Pizza or Buona Forchetta

1:00 PM: Mission Bay chill time or kayak rental

3:00 PM: Belmont Park (optional arcade/rides/beach)

Evening: Casual dinner nearby, relax

📅 October 16 – Barrio Logan & Sunset Views

9:30 AM: Explore Chicano Park murals

10:30 AM: Brunch at Fish Guts or Las Cuatro Milpas

12:30 PM: Cabrillo National Monument

2:30 PM: Sunset Cliffs walk or chill beach time

5:30 PM: Optional: Return to Little Italy for a casual dinner

📅 October 17 – Whale Watching + Coronado

9:00 AM: Whale Watching tour (typically 3–4 hrs)

1:30 PM: Head to Coronado Island (beach or Hotel Del visit)

4:30 PM: Ice cream or light early dinner on Coronado

Evening: Relax at hotel, pack

📅 October 18 – Departure

9:00 AM: Light breakfast near hotel or inside

10:00 AM: Head to airport

12:30 PM: Flight home
